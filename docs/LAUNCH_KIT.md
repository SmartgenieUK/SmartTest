## Core positioning

**One line:** SmartTest is DevGenie's portable, evidence-first testing discipline for AI-assisted software development.

**Problem:** AI can generate an implementation and tests from the same mistaken interpretation, producing a green pipeline that proves very little.

**Promise:** SmartTest helps developers derive verification from intent, trace claims to evidence, test whether important tests can fail, and report uncertainty honestly.

**Boundary:** SmartTest is not a test framework, hosted platform, DevGenie product implementation, or correctness guarantee.

## Response hooks

**“Isn't this just good testing practice?”**

Mostly—and that is intentional. SmartTest packages established engineering discipline into workflows that coding agents can execute consistently. The novelty is not replacing testing practice; it is preventing AI speed from quietly bypassing it.

**“Why not just use coverage and static analysis?”**

Use them. They are valuable evidence providers. They cannot establish that the requirement was interpreted correctly or that the relevant failure modes were selected.

**“Does this replace QA or independent review?”**

No. It helps teams decide when independent derivation or review is warranted and makes missing evidence visible.

**“Is this a DevGenie product?”**

No. SmartTest is a standalone open-source methodology. DevGenie is the originating brand and longer-term product direction.

## Messaging guardrails

- Do not claim SmartTest guarantees correctness, eliminates defects, or replaces QA.
- Do not present test count, coverage, a green pipeline, or an agent review as proof.
- Do not invent adoption, performance, defect-reduction, or popularity statistics.
- Do not describe behavioural probes as broad scientific validation.
- Do not imply endorsement by agent vendors or future evidence providers.
- Keep promotional claims traceable to repository artefacts.
- Do not place repository or GitHub article URLs in initial social posts or public comment replies; fulfil them in a private DM after the `SMARTTEST` comment.
- Be clear that SmartTest is public and open source; do not imply scarcity, exclusivity, or private access.
- Invite falsification and concrete criticism, not just stars.
- Disclose any future affiliate or commercial relationship next to the relevant recommendation.
